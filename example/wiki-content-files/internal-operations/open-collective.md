|description=Transparent money-management platform built specifically for unincorporated
groups: a "Fiscal Host" (typically an existing nonprofit) legally holds funds and handles
compliance and tax filings on the group's behalf, so the group itself never needs to
incorporate or open its own bank account. Every transaction is publicly visible on the
collective's page by default.
|usage_notes=What it does for us: no incorporation needed -- a Fiscal Host (a separate org
that already has legal/tax-exempt status) holds the money and handles compliance on our
behalf, so we can raise, publicly track, and spend funds now, pre-501(c)(3). A host still has
to agree to take us on; each sets its own fee and eligibility policy.

Caveat: as of 2026 there's no easy general-cause US host left on the Open Collective platform
itself -- Open Collective Foundation, formerly the obvious pick, dissolved its fiscal-sponsor
program in 2024. The only host still on-platform, Open Source Collective, is OSS-only (fits
our tooling projects, not the broader mission) -- a general-cause host means looking outside
the Open Collective platform.

If we later incorporate: funds can generally only transfer to another 501(c)(3), not to a
for-profit or an individual. Whether a host will let us use raised funds toward our own
incorporation costs (e.g. attorney fees) is untested -- ask directly, don't assume.

Scale: most-recent figures found in this research pass are from 2019 (platform funds raised
grew from $1.6M to $4M+ that year alone, across thousands of collectives) -- stale by 2026
standards. Check Open Collective's own "Discover" page directly for current totals before
relying on this for a decision.
