# Secure Messaging

End-to-end encrypted direct and small-group messaging for sensitive coordination --
distinct from Slack-like team chat (see [[Category:Team Collaboration]]) and video calls.

These tools keep message *content* private, which is a different job from hiding *where your
traffic goes and who is sending it* -- for that (VPNs, Tor), see
`[[:Category:Privacy & Anonymity]]`{=mediawiki}.

**Order of preference** (same logic as the rest of the wiki):

1. Self-hostable and federated (`[[Element]]`{=mediawiki} / Matrix), where you can run a
   server.
2. Centralized but from a privacy nonprofit (`[[Signal]]`{=mediawiki}) -- best usability,
   minimal metadata, but no self-host fallback.
3. Mainstream platforms only as a last resort.

For high-risk or censored environments, two specialized tools beat both: **SimpleX Chat** (no
user identifier at all) and **Briar** (works offline over Bluetooth/Wi-Fi/Tor -- used in the
2019 Hong Kong and 2022 Iran protests when servers were blocked). Both trade features for that
resilience.

**Rejected for this domain:** WhatsApp (Meta-owned, metadata harvesting) and Telegram (no
default end-to-end encryption) -- encryption or ownership disqualifies them for sensitive work.
