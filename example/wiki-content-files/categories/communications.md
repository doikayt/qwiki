# Communications

Internal and external messaging for the organization.

**On evaluating communication tools:** judge every one on four axes, not just "is it
encrypted":

1. **Content encryption** -- is it end-to-end *by default*, or can the provider read messages?
2. **Metadata** -- what the operator can still see and log (who talks to whom, and when), even
   when content is encrypted.
3. **Infrastructure control** -- can you self-host or federate, or can a single operator cut
   you off, change the rules, or be compelled by a government? Prefer self-hostable/federated,
   then privacy-focused nonprofits, then mainstream platforms.
4. **Endpoint security** -- encryption in transit is moot if the device at either end is
   compromised; that is usually the real attack surface.
