We're an early-stage digital collective building software to help communities survive, and
replace, late-stage disaster capitalism. We look forward to working with organizations and individuals aligned with our values:
- anti-imperialist
- pro-human rights
- pro-tolerance
- pro-free speech
- pro-working-class

Our ideal partners are:
- grassroots community organizations
- nonprofits
- member-led, direct-action-oriented unions
- candidates for public office committed to working-class interests

Our specialties are: user-facing web frameworks, applications, and tooling for TypeScript development, 
-- all built extremely efficiently via AI-native, spec-to-code pipelines.
